Quick answers to common inquiries about our AI cardiac disease detection research

How can I access your research documents?

Research documents are available through our Documents page. For specialized access or collaboration inquiries, please contact us directly through the form above.

What types of collaborations do you offer?

We offer research collaborations, clinical validation partnerships, technology licensing, and academic exchanges. Contact us with your specific proposal.

Can I implement your AI models in my institution?

Yes, we offer implementation guidance and technical support for healthcare institutions. Please reach out with your specific requirements and infrastructure details.
