Literature Survey

Detailed review of cardiac disease detection methodologies and AI approaches

ECG Signal Analysis and Arrhythmia Detection

Comprehensive review of ECG-based arrhythmia detection using deep learning, from traditional feature engineering to modern CNN-LSTM architectures achieving cardiologist-level accuracy on benchmark datasets. References: [3], [7], [12]-[17]

98.6% CNN-LSTM Accuracy
MIT-BIH Benchmark Dataset

Heart Failure Risk Prediction

Analysis of machine learning approaches for HF prediction, focusing on XGBoost performance, probability calibration techniques, and the critical need for reliable risk estimates in clinical decision-making. References: [18], [4], [8], [19]-[22]

85% XGBoost Accuracy
Isotonic Calibration Method

Coronary Artery Disease Risk Prediction

Examination of CAD risk assessment using Framingham dataset, comparing traditional logistic regression with ensemble methods like ExtraTrees, and the importance of explainable AI for clinical trust. References: [23]-[27], [9], [10]

98.05% ExtraTrees Accuracy
SHAP/LIME Explainability

Left Ventricular Hypertrophy Detection

Review of automated echocardiographic LVH detection using spatiotemporal deep learning architectures like X3D-M, with Grad-CAM++ for visual explainability in cardiac imaging AI. References: [28]-[33]

Prohibitive Costs

Commercial ECG monitoring solutions cost USD 200-2,000+, creating significant barriers to accessibility in developing regions.

Our Solution: IoT hardware at $20/unit

Black-Box Models

Most cardiac AI systems lack transparency, making it difficult for clinicians to trust and understand diagnostic decisions.

Our Solution: SHAP & LIME explainability

Single-Pathology Focus

Existing systems focus on individual cardiac conditions, missing opportunities for comprehensive cardiovascular health assessment.

Our Solution: 4-in-1 detection platform

Resource Constraints

Our Solution: Clinical Dashboard
